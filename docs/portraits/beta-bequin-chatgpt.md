# Alizebeth and Beta Bequin: portrait provenance

The first Beta Bequin trial was generated with the published *Pariah* and *Penitent* covers as visual references. Because that made it derivative of third-party artwork, neither the trial nor the cover images are part of this repository. Alizebeth's earlier portrait was dropped too, since it inherited from the trial.

Both characters were regenerated (v3) with ChatGPT Images:

- Identity comes only from the books' textual descriptions. Alizebeth is tall, dark-haired and strikingly beautiful, and juvenat treatment keeps her looking about thirty. Beta is her clone: a tall, slender 23–24-year-old with black hair and large dark eyes.
- Style reference: [Kara Swole's portrait](sources/kara-swole.chatgpt-background-v2.png), for brushwork and lighting only.
- Beta was generated from the new Alizebeth portrait as a facial-identity reference, so the clones share one face.
- Costumes, hairstyles, poses and settings are original and avoid the cover designs.
