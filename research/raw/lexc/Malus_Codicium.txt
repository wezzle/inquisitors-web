				# Malus Codicium
					From Warhammer 40k - Lexicanum
										Jump to: navigation, search
***Chaos Portal***
The **Malus Codicium** is a dangerous tome of daemonic knowledge. It was highly sought after by several individuals, most notable among whom were Inquisitor Helgrund[4][5], who sought to destroy it, and Pontius Glaw, who required its vast sum of knowledge to acquire the Daemon-king Yssarile's barque for himself.[3]
It was in the possession of the renegade Inquisitor Quixos until he was killed by Gregor Eisenhorn. Instead of handing the book over to the Inquisition, Eisenhorn hid it for his own use, and through it learned how to bind daemonhosts and create psychic thralls.[1][3]
The book was finally destroyed in a confrontation between Eisenhorn and Pontius Glaw on the planet Ghül.[3]
Long before this, during the Horus Heresy, a spell from it was used by Menkaura of the Thousand Sons to bind a shard of Magnus the Red into Lemuel Gaumon.[2]
