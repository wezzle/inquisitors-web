## Overview
Sameter is a centre for agro-chemical production and used to be the pre-eminent world of the subsector, before the capital was moved to Thracian Primaris some time before **241.M41**. After that it was in terminal decline, as competition from the hives of Thracian Primaris had forced the planetary governor to relax controls on pollution in order to compete. Instead of resurrecting the local economy, it lead to a further decline in production.[1]
Uber Aemos noted during an Inquisitorial investigation in **241.M41** that Sameter had a notable, but not unusual, level of criminal activity. This primarily took the form of narcotics trafficking and mob violence (the latter usually linked to the former), but *"...nothing really markedly heretical."*[1]
## History
**Spoiler!**
The following paragraphs contain spoilers for:
Missing in Action (Short Story))
Prior to the investigation Aemos was part of, the last time Sameter had been scrutinised by the Inquisition was in **210.M41**.[1][Note 1] This was due to the discovery of a Rogue Psyker active in Sameter's capital city.[1]
In **241.M41** Sameter was the subject of two investigations by Inquisitor Gregor Eisenhorn. The first of these (a suspected link between an anti-Imperial organisation on Hesperus) and a trade federation on Sameter) proved to be a dead end. The second, however, was more interesting.[1]
Eisenhorn and his retinue were about to leave Sameter after closing the aforementioned case when he was contacted by Eskeen Hansaard, Minister of Security of the city of Urbitane. The city's Arbites garrison had uncovered evidence of a series of ritual murders that led Eisenhorn to believe that an heretical cult was active on Sameter. After further investigation, however, it transpired that the "cult" was in fact made up of veteran Imperial Guardsmen who, suffering from extreme mental trauma from their experiences, believed that Sameter had been infiltrated by the forces of Chaos. The remaining Guardsmen were rounded up by Eisenhorn and Sameter's Arbites, although some of them resisted and subsequently fought to their deaths.[1]
Even years later, authorities were still finding the bodies of murder victims.[1]
## Geography
Sameter has at least two major conurbations; the planet's capital city is Aquitane and its second city is Urbitane.[1]
### Years and Seasons
The people of Sameter make use of a local calendar. One of its months is **Aquiarae**, which has at least 24 days, and this is followed by **Sagittar**, which has at least 13. In 241.M41 one of Sameter's solstices was three days after the 13th of Sagittar.[1]
## Military
The planet is known to provide regiments for the Imperial Guard. Notably, all Guard regiments from Sameter are mixed-sex regiments.[1]
One of the regiments raised on Sameter was the Ninth Sameter Infantry.[1]
