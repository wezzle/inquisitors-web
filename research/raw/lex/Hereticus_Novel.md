## Cover Description


Hunted by his former allies as a [radical](/wiki/Radical) and enemy of the [Imperium](/wiki/Imperium), [Inquisitor](/wiki/Inquisitor) [Gregor Eisenhorn](/wiki/Gregor_Eisenhorn) must fight to prove that he remains loyal as he tracks down a dangerous [heretic](/wiki/Heretic) whom the Inquisition believes dead, the dread former Inquisitor [Quixos](/wiki/Quixos). As he grows more desperate for victory, Eisenhorn uses ever darker means to achieve his goals, but how far can he go using the weapons of the enemy until he becomes that very enemy, and no different to the traitor he hunts?[[1]](#fn_1)



## Plot Summary


386.[M41](/wiki/M41). Due to health concerns, [Lord Inquisitor](/wiki/Lord_Inquisitor) [Plebas Alessandro Rorken](/wiki/Plebas_Alessandro_Rorken) is unable to supervise the trial of heretics on [Durer](/wiki/Durer), and Eisenhorn must conduct the examination in his stead, shadowed by newly elevated inquisitor [Bastian Verveuk](/wiki/Bastian_Verveuk).[[2a]](#fn_2a)



#### Part One: Durer


Eisenhorn abruptly suspends the Court of Examination at Durer to go after [Fayde Thuring](/wiki/Fayde_Thuring), who killed [Midas Betancore](/wiki/Midas_Betancore) 80 years earlier.[[2b]](#fn_2b) Joined by [Poul Rassi](/mediawiki/index.php?title=Poul_Rassi&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1) of the [Ordo Malleus](/wiki/Ordo_Malleus) and, to his dismay, Verveuk, Eisenhorn boards his gun-cutter where he and members of his retinue are briefed by [Godwyn Fischig](/wiki/Godwyn_Fischig), who found a trail leading to a disused [PDF](/wiki/Planetary_Defence_Forces) listening station on one of Durer’s polar islands.[[2c]](#fn_2c)


Investigating the station, the inquisitorial team prevails in a firefight but is incapacitated by a psychic resonance as Thuring reveals the object of his machinations: a functional [Chaos Warlord Titan](/wiki/Chaos_Warlord_Titan) named *[Cruor Vult](/wiki/Cruor_Vult)*.[[2d]](#fn_2d)
Evading the god-engine as best as they can, the inquisitors come up with a plan: to establish a psychic link with *Cruor Vult* and channel the null power of *[Alizebeth Bequin](/wiki/Alizebeth_Bequin)* into the enemy's mind to annihilate it.[[2e]](#fn_2e) However, Bequin is grievously wounded by the backlash, and the gun-cutter brought down by the Titan's weapons; Eisenhorn then resorts to using the *[Malus Codicium](/wiki/Malus_Codicium)* he purloined from the renegade Quixos forty years earlier.[[2f]](#fn_2f) He unleashes [Cherubael](/wiki/Cherubael), which destroys the Titan before turning on the inquisitor, who manages to bind the [Daemon](/wiki/Daemon) to the body of the dying Verveuk.[[2g]](#fn_2g)



#### Part Two: Gudrun


On the way back to [Gudrun](/wiki/Gudrun), Eisenhorn contends with Cherubael’s mind tricks and his own fear of straying from the path of the [Emperor](/wiki/Emperor). At his estate, he organizes an auto-seance so [Medea Betancore](/wiki/Medea_Betancore) can commune with the psy-memory of her father, but realizes the house is under attack.[[2h]](#fn_2h) Faced with a carefully planned attack and resourceful foes, Eisenhorn manages to escape with Eleena Koi of the [Distaff](/wiki/Distaff) and [Uber Aemos](/wiki/Uber_Aemos).[[2i]](#fn_2i) They reach a hidden turbofan flier and go back to the House, rescuing Medea and getting shot down in the ensuing chase.[[2j]](#fn_2j)


Eisenhorn hijacks an enemy speeder and makes the surviving mercenary a prisoner before heading to Ravello, where Dr. [Crezia Berschilde](/mediawiki/index.php?title=Crezia_Berschilde&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1) takes the team in and tends to a wounded Medea. The inquisitor then convinces her to help him interrogate the mercenary, who turns out to be a [Vessorine Janissary](/wiki/Vessorine_Janissaries) by the name of Vammeko Tarl, hired along with others by one [Marla Tarray](/mediawiki/index.php?title=Marla_Tarray&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1) on behalf of [Khanjar the Sharp](/wiki/Pontius_Glaw).[[2k]](#fn_2k) While researching these new threads, they learn that the raid on Gudrun was only one in a series of simultaneous attacks on Eisenhorn’s assets—and Tarl escapes.[[2l]](#fn_2l)


Once more on the run, Eisenhorn’s team—along with Berschilde—boards a luxury train to New Gevae, Gudrun’s southern capital. Contacted by one of his surviving agents whom he'll meet there, Eisenhorn goes through the evidence once again and has a break in the case of Tarray's identity.[[2m]](#fn_2m) Aemos and Eisenhorn discuss the new evidence that **Pontius Glaw** is the mastermind behind their misfortunes. Medea regains consciousness, and is brought up to date; soon after, the train abruptly stops and armed figures board it.[[2n]](#fn_2n) Eisenhorn dispatches the Janissaries and their leader, and asserts his inquisitorial status to deflect the local authorities’ investigation. Once in New Gevae, he finds out his agent’s message was a fake and sets a trap of his own through a thrall, unleashing a warp vortex that annihilates Tarray, daughter to Pontius Glaw.[[2o]](#fn_2o)



#### Part Three: Ghül


Aboard the *Essene*, discomfort with Eisenhorn’s new methods is growing. At [Hubris](/wiki/Hubris), Fischig learns of the recent events and has a fall out with the inquisitor, who moves on without him.[[2p]](#fn_2p) On [Promody](/mediawiki/index.php?title=Promody&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1), the team reunites with [Kara Swole](/wiki/Kara_Swole) and [Harlon Nayl](/wiki/Harlon_Nayl), who were extracted by [Gideon Ravenor](/wiki/Gideon_Ravenor). Eisenhorn’s former [Interrogator](/wiki/Interrogator) informs him that, thanks to pacts made with the [Aeldari](/wiki/Aeldari), he has perfected his divination skills and found that there was no future to divine six months from now.[[2q]](#fn_2q)


Promody was one of seven planets conquered by Daemons long before the birth of [Humankind](/wiki/Human), and Glaw has been looking for the capital of this empire: [Ghül](/wiki/Gh%C3%BCl), known to the Imperium as 5213X. An enlarged auto-seance reveals eldritch writing that Aemos sets to translating. Using the *Malus Codicium*, the savant receives the help of Cherubael and tells Eisenhorn of the Daemon-King Yssarile, whose ship lies on Ghül.[[2r]](#fn_2r)


While the *Essene* is serviced at [Jeganda](/mediawiki/index.php?title=Jeganda&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1), Eisenhorn stumbles upon Fischig, who is being used by Inquisitors [Heldane](/wiki/Golesh_Constantine_Pheppos_Heldane) and [Osma](/wiki/Leonid_Osma) to catch Eisenhorn. Cherubael intervenes, allowing Eisenhorn to escape, and gets bound in Fischig’s body.[[2s]](#fn_2s) On Ghül, Eisenhorn finally faces Pontius Glaw who now occupies a mechanical body constructed by [Geard Bure](/wiki/Geard_Bure). Distracting his opponent by setting the *Malus Codicium* ablaze, the inquisitor dispatches him with Barbarisater.[[2t]](#fn_2t)


Ghül is subjected to Exterminatus in 392.[M41](/wiki/M41). Eisenhorn’s continued service to the Inquisition is a matter of speculation.[[2u]](#fn_2u)

### Inaccuracies in Cover Description


Despite stating that Eisenhorn is hunting Quixos, that does not occur in this book; rather, it occurs in the previous book *[Malleus](/wiki/Malleus_(Novel))*. ***Hereticus*** details Eisenhorn's hunt to bring down a resurrected [Pontius Glaw](/wiki/Pontius_Glaw) who has gained a body and now seeks to achieve godhood.
