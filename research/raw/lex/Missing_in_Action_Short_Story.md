***Missing in Action*** is a short story in the [Eisenhorn novel series](/wiki/Eisenhorn_(Novel_Series)) by [Dan Abnett](/wiki/Dan_Abnett).







## Cover Description[[1]](#fn_1)


A series of mysterious murders in [a hive city](/mediawiki/index.php?title=Urbitaine&amp;action=edit&amp;redlink=1) pique the interest of [Inquisitor](/wiki/Inquisitor) [Gregor Eisenhorn](/wiki/Gregor_Eisenhorn). There's a commonality between them all that suggests something deeper than simply random acts of a crazed killer – but what could be behind the brutal slayings, and what will it mean for the safety of the entire city?
