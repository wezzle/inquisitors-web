					From Warhammer 40k - Lexicanum

***Publications Portal***
The **Eisenhorn** trilogy by Dan Abnett is a collection of three novels -  **Xenos**, **Malleus**, and **Hereticus** - featuring Inquisitor Gregor Eisenhorn. 
A later omnibus edition contains these three novels and also features two short stories, *Missing In Action)* and *Backcloth For A Crown Additional)*. 
A collection of audio shorts, Thorn and Talon), was released in 2012.
