***[Publications Portal](/wiki/Portal:Publications)***

The **Eisenhorn** trilogy by [Dan Abnett](/wiki/Dan_Abnett) is a collection of three novels -  **Xenos**, **Malleus**, and **Hereticus** - featuring [Inquisitor](/wiki/Inquisitor) [Gregor Eisenhorn](/wiki/Gregor_Eisenhorn). 


A later omnibus edition contains these three novels and also features two short stories, *[Missing In Action](/wiki/Missing_in_Action_(Short_Story))* and *[Backcloth For A Crown Additional](/wiki/Backcloth_For_A_Crown_Additional_(Short_Story))*. 


A collection of audio shorts, [Thorn and Talon](/wiki/Thorn_and_Talon_(Audio_Book)), was released in 2012.
